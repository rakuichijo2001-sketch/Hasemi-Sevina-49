### Interim router observation

The earlier user-provided screenshot showed 21,842 violations at 90% of the
first detailed-route optimization iteration. The now available job log shows
that subsequent iterations reduced the intermediate count to 18,778 after
iteration 12, but the next iteration was interrupted. Neither count is final
sign-off DRC. Dense placement and slow violation reduction support investigating
area and routing pressure in 3x2; they do not identify a specific RTL block as
the root cause.
