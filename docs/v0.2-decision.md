## Decision

**The current 3x2 implementation has not demonstrated physical fit.** Routing
had 18,778 violations after 12 optimization iterations and the job was
cancelled before a final route result. This is a measured routing blocker;
it does not by itself prove that no 3x2 implementation can fit.

Keep the TinyQV integration as an experimental branch. Do not claim 3x2 fit,
alter verification criteria, or increase the fab tile allocation without an
explicit design decision. Diagnose the routing hotspot and cell/repair costs
from this exact-SHA run before choosing a small, measured RTL experiment.

## Next decision step

Use the completed job log to characterize congestion and select a 3x2 candidate.
Close the listed QSPI direction/contention and integrated-arbitration tests,
then rerun RTL, synthesis and hardening on the candidate SHA before choosing:

- **FIT** only with clean final route/signoff and useful timing/utilization
  margin;
- **FIT WITH REDUCTION** only after one evidence-backed reduction passes the
  complete RTL/GLS/physical gates;
- **DOES NOT FIT / TOO RISKY** if routing does not converge, critical timing or
  signoff fails, or fit depends on weakened verification/constraints.
